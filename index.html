A HOME BUILT ON HOPE

What began with seven children became a lifeline for hundreds.

Rehoboth Blessed Assurance Community Based Organization was founded in 2012 by Pauline Wairimu Wathimu. With no regular donors or sponsors, the organization has continued through the generosity of well-wishers, churches, neighbours and friends.

In 2013, a devastating house fire destroyed everything. Rehoboth continued. Today, the mission remains simple: stand with vulnerable children and families and give them dignity, care and the opportunity for a brighter future.

MEET THE FOUNDER

“We kept moving forward by grace and faith.”

Pauline Wairimu Wathimu founded Rehoboth in 2012 with seven children. Through hardship, a devastating fire and years without regular sponsorship, she and the Rehoboth community have continued showing up for children who need care.

Rehoboth's vision is a compassionate community where every vulnerable child and family has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope and a brighter future.
